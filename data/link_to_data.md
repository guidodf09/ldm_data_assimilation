Find the geomodels (`.h5`), the true cases and the simulated rates (used for the surrogate error and training) in the `data/` folder of [https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1vhaRdzOv56gIDrDwPhm8xDW6S-g_nhXB?usp=sharing](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1vhaRdzOv56gIDrDwPhm8xDW6S-g_nhXB?usp=sharing)

Place them as:

```
data/
  geomodels_128_paper.h5
  true{1,2,3}_model.npy
  true{1,2,3}_rates.npy
  rates/
    all_data_all_times_part1.npy
    all_data_all_times_part2.npy
    all_data_all_times_part3.npy
```
